# Publish rules - Facebook post, YouTube package, thumbnail

## The authority: the Toons Listing Formula

All post and description copy follows `Toons Listing Formula.txt` in `Desktop\Toons Listings\` (live copy: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/af2c4b9b-6a40-47d8-a443-0156a0b83e2e). Read it each time; if it disagrees with this file, the Formula wins. The essentials:

- Voice: professional, specific, approachable, confident, local. No hype ("premium, best-in-class", "adventure awaits"), no fake urgency ("act now", "before someone grabs it").
- Claim-check every statement: verified fact / manufacturer claim / restricted. Keep restricted or unknown facts out and ask. Brand restrictions apply (e.g. Xpress: no commercial/fleet eligibility; Tahoe: no MSRP or savings).
- No price in post or description text, and never cost or margin. Disclose condition issues.
- Seven blocks in order: Hook, Fit, Proof, Feel, Specs (every verified feature on this unit), Warranty (only if verified), Next step.
- Listing wording is checked with Ernie or Harbor before publishing: present drafts for approval.

## Facebook post

- Short and image-led: Hook + Feel in 2-3 sentences, specs as one quick line; casual but Toons; no price.
- Always toonsusa.com, 918-473-5055, "ask for <salesperson>", Toons Eufaula, I-40 Exit 259 off OK-150, "Family. Water. Fun.", and the pre-owned and consignment message.
- Give a full Formula post (recommended) and a short Reel caption.
- Remind Ernie: upload the .srt, put the price in the Marketplace price field, upload the 1080p FINAL from a computer or with HD uploads on.

## YouTube package (one doc per boat)

1. Upload checklist: the `_YouTube_1440p.mp4` for YouTube, the 1080p `_FINAL.mp4` for Facebook, the .srt, the vertical thumbnail. Include a duration check (the wrong file was uploaded once). Captions: Subtitles > Add > Upload file > **"With timing"**, English, then play the opening to check sync.
2. Upload quality: upload the 1440p file; wait for HD processing before judging.
3. Title: 100 characters or fewer; year, model, motor, hours, Pre-Owned/Consignment, Toons Eufaula; no price.
4. Description: Formula blocks, contact lines, feature checklist, pre-owned/consignment and shipping lines, CHAPTERS (from 0:00, 3 or more, each 10 s or longer, timed from the FINAL edit; re-time whenever the edit changes), location, tagline, socials, 3 hashtags.
5. Tags. 6. Pinned comment (helpful, no urgency).
7. Settings: not made for kids, Autos & Vehicles, English, location, playlist (Xpress Boats Walkthroughs for Xpress; Pre-Owned & Consignment Boats for used units).
8. Shorts notes (no chapters/end screens/cards; use Related video; state the real final duration) and a "To confirm" list.

Playlist descriptions live in `_Brand\YouTube_playlist_descriptions.txt` (Pre-Owned & Consignment Boats done). A new playlist gets one in the same style, added to that file. Studio: Content > Playlists > pencil > Description.

## Thumbnail

- Vertical 1080x1920 for Shorts (16:9 gets cropped): boat photo on top (~860-900 px) with the real Toons logo on a white pill, white rule, blue `#0A56A1` panel, eyebrow (PRE-OWNED / CONSIGNMENT / JUST ARRIVED), 3-line Anton title, white box with hours or a key spec (no price), "Toons Eufaula - Lake Eufaula, OK".
- Photo: sharpness scan (edge variance on frames every 0.12-0.25 s) near the best whole-boat daylight shot; no registration numbers; light grade. Ask for a real listing photo when footage is dim.
- Optional 16:9 in the Canva style ("2027 Xpress Bayou 180 YouTube thumbnail"). Canva uploads are blocked from the cloud workspace: copy the design, edit the text, Ernie swaps the photo.
