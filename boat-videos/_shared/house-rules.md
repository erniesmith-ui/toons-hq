# House rules - Ernie's feedback, so no video goes back for the same problem twice

Every rule here came from a video Ernie sent back. When he gives new feedback, add it here the same day.

## The two core rules

1. **Play the footage the way it was shot.** Every cut inside a continuous shot was rejected, however it was hidden. Fix problems by choosing where the video starts and ends, and with cards and the end card, not with cuts.
2. **Export at full quality.** Over-sharpened, twice-compressed, badly color-tagged files looked bad after upload. Follow the export settings in the [edit recipe](edit-recipe.md) exactly.

## Editing

- Never cut inside a continuous shot. Edit only where the footage already changes shot (walkaround clip ends, inside clip begins).
- No dissolves, punch-in zooms, freeze frames, Ken Burns stills, cutaways with the voice running over them, or speed changes. (Hurricane v4 was "unwatchable"; v5 with stills was worse.)
- No hard or abrupt cuts, glitches or stutter.
- If the intro has a slip, start the video at the first clean sentence after it. Name, phone and website go on cards.
- Leave small asides in (Kevin's coffee cup stayed). Ask before any cut that lands inside a shot.
- End on the salesperson's own sign-off ("see you at the gate"). Nothing plays after it except the end card. Lines said after the sign-off become cards earlier in the video.
- Fix a salesperson's mistake (e.g. a consignment boat called "used/trade-in", a factory option called "non-factory") only if it can be done without a visible cut, with a card; otherwise ask Ernie.
- Keep it under 3:00 (YouTube Short). If it runs longer, ask Ernie what to drop.

## Graphics

- One card on screen at a time. Never two overlays at once.
- Cards sit in one consistent, clear spot and appear on the line that names the feature. They must not look randomly placed.
- A card tied to a gesture goes where the person points (the phone card where Kevin points) and shows only while they point.
- Never cover the salesperson's face, the motor or the Toons sign.

## Audio

- Clean up loud breathing and uneven background noise; speech level even across shots.

## Thumbnails

- Vertical 9:16 for Shorts, with a good, clear photo of the boat.

## Copy

- Follow the Toons Listing Formula ([publish rules](publish-rules.md)): no price, no hype, no fake urgency. (The Xpress H18 posts broke this; don't repeat it.)

## Goal

World-class, high-end feel; clearly "a Toons video"; hits the boat's top highlights; subtly shows where to buy. Ernie wants per-video editing down from ~6 hours to about 15 minutes of his time.
